# Timah Feedback

- The ability to drag the timers around doesn't work well on mobile. Works okay on desktop for some reason. I think we remove the drag/drop feature of the timers and replace it with a move up/down feature instead.

- When running a longer timer, I noticed the phone would go to sleep. I think it also caused the pod to suspend due to "inactivity" even though the timer was running.  Let's add a feature that keeps the screen active on mobile and keeps the pod alive while a timer is running

- Can the volume of the sounds be increased at all?  I like to listen to music while I workout but I'm okay with the beeps coming through clearer.
